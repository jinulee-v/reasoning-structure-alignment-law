Fetched 2026-09-01.  The `whole` split is the corpus (300 cases); `hard` is a
subset of 80, flagged per case in INDEX.json rather than stored separately.
Files under airline/ are verbatim from those parquets; never edit them.

  airline/cases/<id>.pl      the case's own reference Prolog program
  airline/meta/<id>.json     text, question, label, hard flag
  airline/statutes/          section1_non_textual, identical across all 300
                             cases and stored once

DeonticBench's other four domains are not here.  sara_numeric and sara_binary
are SARA, already ported under sara_catala/.  housing was not requested.
uscis-aao was downloaded, measured and dropped: its 242 reference programs
share no formalization to port --- the mean pairwise Jaccard overlap of their
predicate vocabularies is 0.021 and 94.4% of the 6,080 predicates they use
appear in exactly one case --- so there is no statute there to write once and
test against, only 242 unrelated programs.  See deontic_scripts/README.md.
